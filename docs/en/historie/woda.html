Dobra, Chyszówki, Półrzeczki, Jurków · 1712–1923 · a new story from the registers

Water

The streams coming down from Mogielica and Łopień drove mills and sawmills. The registers also recorded the people the water took.

Translated from the Polish original with the help of Claude. I give quotations from the registers in the original, with a translation.

The villages of the parish lie in the valleys of streams flowing down from Mogielica, Łopień, and Ćwilin. Water gave life and drove mills and sawmills, but it also took people.

The icon from Jurków

According to tradition, a flood carried the icon of the Transfiguration down from Jurków. It was found on the “kamieniec,” that is, the stony riverbank, below the church in Dobra. The parish priest Wojciech Juraszewski set up a separate altar for it in the church. Father Edward Wojtusiak, who described this, gave the year 1712 in one work and the years 1683–1690 in another. The icon has survived to this day, as told in the story “The Icon from Jurków”. The parish priest himself is the subject of the story “The Heaped Bushel”.

More than a hundred years later, in July 1825, the priest recorded a death in a flood in the death register of Chyszówki.

Children by the water

The registers record a dozen or so drownings. The victims were almost always children who were playing by a stream or fell into a well:

  • Dobra · 1810Katarzyna Forstek, aged 7.
  • Dobra · 1831Katarzyna Przybytek, aged 9.
  • Półrzeczki · 1833Katarzyna Kuchta, aged 4½.
  • Półrzeczki · 1841Regina Walczak, aged 2.
  • Chyszówki · 1845Sebastian Sędzioł, aged 8, drowned in a well.
  • Dobra · 1846Franciszka Miśkowiec, aged 12, in January.
  • Półrzeczki · 1849Jan Walczak, aged 5, in a well.
  • Dobra · 1850Jan Kudlacz, aged 14, drowned in a stream.
  • Chyszówki · 1858–1870Andrzej Czopek, aged 4, Tomasz Pałka, aged 5, and two-year-old Anna Gąsior.
  • Dobra · 1923Anna Chalcarz, aged 9. “Utopiła się przypadkiem,” she drowned by accident, wrote the priest, by then in Polish.

Adults drowned too: in 1817, 76-year-old Antoni Miśkowiec of Dobra, and in December 1841, 30-year-old Sebastian Kuchta of Półrzeczki.

Water killed at home as well. In 1894 two-and-a-half-year-old Maria Dudzik of Jurków was scalded by boiling water.

How certain is this

  • CertainThe deaths cited. The entries were checked against the scans.
  • ProbableThe flood that brought the icon from Jurków. We know of it from published accounts, not from the registers, and its date is uncertain.

What we don’t know

  • How many drownings the priest recorded without giving the cause.
  • When exactly the flood that brought the icon from Jurków took place.
