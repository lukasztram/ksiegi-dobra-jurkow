Dobra · 1689–1929 · a new story from the registers

The Organists of Dobra

The organist played in church, led the singing, often taught the children, and stood as a witness at weddings. The registers know the organists of Dobra by name from the late 17th century on.

Translated from the Polish original with the help of Claude. I give quotations from the registers in the original, with a translation.

The organist was an important person in the parish. He played the organ and led the singing in church, and often taught the children as well. The registers usually call him “organarius.” We meet him in them most often as a godfather or a wedding witness, because he was at the church every day.

From Mielecki to Szewczyk

The first organist of Dobra whom we know by name is Jan Mielecki. In October 1689 he held a child at its baptism.

A few years earlier, in 1685–1687, the registers mention Tomasz Mielecki, rector of the school in Dobra, who appears in the story “The Marshal, the Land, and the School”. The school and the organ were in the hands of men with the same surname.

These are the organists the registers mention most often:

  • 1689–1697Jan Mielecki
  • 1722–1731Wawrzyniec Józef Myczkowski
  • 1738–1743Marcin Zagajski
  • 1757–1762Sebastian Miernicki
  • 1765–1785Jakub Świetliński
  • 1788–1822Antoni Stramski
  • 1827–1881Mateusz Rudnicki
  • 1882–1902Kazimierz Mendalski
  • 1904–1907Szymon Malaga
  • 1907–1929Ignacy Szewczyk

The years are the first and last mentions in the transcribed registers, not the beginning and end of service. In between, the registers mention several more organists whose names were written unclearly.

The Stramski family

Antoni Stramski played in Dobra from at least 1788 to 1822. He stood godfather to more than thirty children, and his wife Magdalena godmother to more than twenty. They often stood as godparents together, recorded as “Organarius Dobrensis” and “uxor ejus,” his wife. Antoni was also a witness at ten weddings.

The Rudnicki family of house no. 98

Mateusz Rudnicki played the longest. The registers record him as organist from 1827 to 1881, that is, for more than half a century. He was also the teacher at the trivial school, that is, the elementary school, in Dobra. His father, Piotr Rudnicki, had already been a teacher.

Mateusz and his wife Helena, née Wróblewska, lived in house no. 98. In 1829–1857 they had seventeen children baptized there. At least three died as small children. The godmother of five of them was the wife of the leaseholder of Dobra, Kunegunda Kisielewska, who appears in the story “The Manor Official of Dobra”.

The organist’s son

Mateusz’s son Ludwik was born on August 6, 1834, the Feast of the Transfiguration, and became a priest. He was a curate in Międzybrodzie. Ill with consumption, he came to his parents in Dobra to recover, and died there on July 8, 1875, in the sixteenth year of his priesthood.

Below the entry the priest listed the clergy who came to the funeral. There were a dozen or so: parish priests and curates from Mszana, Kasina, Skrzydlna, Limanowa, and other parishes, and Cistercians from Szczyrzyc. The sermons were preached by the parish priest of Tymbark and the Cistercian Father Maksymilian.

After the Rudnickis

Mateusz Rudnicki appears in the registers as organist for the last time in 1881. A year later the organist was Kazimierz Mendalski, whom we meet as a wedding witness during the building of the railroad, as told in the story “The Railroad and the Steam Saw”. Ignacy Szewczyk, organist in the early 20th century, witnessed at least twenty weddings.

How certain is this

  • CertainEntries from 1689, 1722, 1738, 1757, 1765, 1788, 1827, 1844, 1875, 1882, 1906, and 1907, checked against the scans.
  • ProbableThe organists’ years of service, since these are only the first and last mentions in the transcribed registers. The numbers of baptisms and weddings were counted by machine.
  • To be checkedThe names of early organists, which the registers spell in different ways, for example Myczkowski and Myszkowski.

What we don’t know

  • Who played in the years when the registers name no organist, for example in the early 18th century.
  • When Mateusz Rudnicki died. The death register of Dobra for the 1880s has not yet been transcribed.
