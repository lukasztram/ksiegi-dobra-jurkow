On the first page of the oldest surviving parish register of Dobra, the parish priest wrote down where it all begins.

The church in Dobra burned down on July 2, 1678, the feast of the Visitation of the Blessed Virgin Mary, at two o’clock in the morning. The fire was started through the carelessness of the schoolmaster and the organist. The organ, the bells, the chasubles, four chalices, the ciboria, the monstrance, and two silver crosses all burned.

The older registers most likely burned too. That is why the history of the parish’s families begins in the registers only in the summer of 1678. The parish priest Marcin Franciszek Dąbrowski recorded the first baptism from Jurków in the new register on August 14, 1678. He baptized Ewa, daughter of Jan and Anna. The father’s surname can be read only uncertainly, as Halcarzyk. It may be Chalcarz, a family well known in Jurków.

Half a century later, several months of 1733 went missing from the baptism register. The parish priest explained why.

Why the curate left by night and what he did with the records is not known. Those baptisms were never recovered. The children baptized in the spring of 1733 grew up, worked, and started families, but they are not in the baptism register.

Notes like these explain the gaps in the registers. When months or years are missing from the record of baptisms, it was the register that went missing, not the people.

How certain is this

  • CertainThe church burned down in 1678, and the register begins after the fire. The parish priest’s note says so.
  • To be checkedThe curate’s surname and the surname of Ewa’s family from 1678 are read with uncertainty.

What we don’t know

  • Whether any register from before 1678 survived elsewhere, for example as copies at the bishop’s curia.
  • Why the curate Jawernowski left the parish by night.
