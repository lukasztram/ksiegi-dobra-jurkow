The priest marked twins with a single word: “gemelli.” Sometimes he wrote them on one line, sometimes on two, one under the other, with the same date and the same parents. The baptism registers of the parish contain about four hundred such births, roughly one in 65 (as of November 2026). This rate, about one and a half percent, does not change from the early 18th century to the 1920s. Triplets do not appear in the registers even once.

Twins were two girls, two boys, or a boy and a girl about equally often. Usually each child got its own godparents.

Names from the calendar, in pairs

The twins of 1724 were born just before St. Joseph’s Day, March 19. St. Joachim’s day was then celebrated on March 20, the day after. There are more such pairs in the registers. Twins were given the names of two saints whose days fell close to the date of birth:

  • JanuaryAgnieszka and Sebastian: January 20 and 21. Twins were given these names in 1703, 1781, and 1812.
  • MayStanisław and Zofia: May 8 and 15. Twins born in May 1742, 1784, and 1801.
  • JunePiotr and Paweł, June 29. Twins from June 1818 and 1865.
  • NovemberAndrzej and Mikołaj: November 30 and December 6. Twins from November 1743, 1744, and 1811.
  • DecemberTomasz and Wiktoria: December 21 and 23. Twins from December 1811 and 1842.

How names were chosen in general is told in the story {{tytul:imiona}}.

Twins twice

In some houses twins were born more than once. Wojciech Jania and Urszula Jania, née Czyrnek, of Jurków no. 37 baptized Anna and Marianna in April 1845, and Tomasz and Jan in December 1850. Józef Stokłosa and Kunegunda Stokłosa, née Gąsior, of Dobra no. 66 had twins in October 1889, Szymon and Marianna, and then again in March 1901, Józef and Wojciech.

The first year

Twins are often born too early and are smaller. The registers show what this cost. In 1787–1910, in the villages for which we also have death registers, more than one twin in three died in the first year of life. Among children from single births, roughly one in five died before their first birthday in the same period, that is, half as often. In 28 pairs out of 141, neither of the two survived. Almost one in three of the twins who died did not live to be a week old. How infants died is told in the story {{tytul:pierwszy-rok}}.

How certain is this

  • CertainThe entries about twins quoted here. The transcriptions were checked against the scans.
  • ProbableThe figures, as of November 2026, from the registers of all nine villages. They were counted by machine from the transcriptions, and deaths were linked to baptisms by name, parents, house, and the age of the deceased. Some deaths may have been missed, so mortality was more likely higher than lower.

What we don’t know

  • How many twins died right after birth without being baptized in church. The priest sometimes recorded such children only in the death register.
  • How many women died giving birth to twins.
