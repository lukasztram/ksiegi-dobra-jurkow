Opposite house no. 5 in Jurków, where my ancestors, the Podgórni family, lived for a hundred and forty-five years, the Chapel of the Holy Family stands in Szyrszenie. It was built of stone in 1826, with a tower at the front. A painting of the Holy Family hangs in the altar. To this day Mass is said here on Pentecost Monday, and in May the neighbors come for the May devotions. On May 30, 2026, the parish of Dobra celebrated the chapel’s two-hundredth anniversary.

Relics from the bishop

A year after it was built, on July 16, 1827, the Bishop of Tarnów, Grzegorz Tomasz Ziegler, consecrated a portable altar for the chapel, that is, a portable altar stone with relics of Saints Innocent and Valentine. His handwritten Latin inscription has survived on the slab. Below it is a second one, from August 23, 1848: Bishop Józef Grzegorz Wojtarowicz, during his visitation of the parish of Dobra, sealed the relics again. Relics were expensive. They point to wealthy founders, or to someone who knew how to obtain them.

Who built it

According to local tradition, written down by a regional historian, the chapel was built by a father from the Podgórni family in thanksgiving to God because one of his sons had become a priest. The historian could not establish who this priest was. I tried in the registers.

In the 1820s three Podgórni families lived in the neighboring houses no. 5 and 6. In house no. 5, the brothers Józef, with his wife Agnieszka, née Trzopek, and Melchior, with his wife Anna, née Styś. In house no. 6, Jan with Marianna, née Pałka. In 1826 none of their sons could have been priests yet. The older sons of Jan and Katarzyna of house no. 5 could have been: Jan, born in 1784, Szymon in 1789, and Andrzej in 1794. The Jurków registers do not record what became of them. Their father, however, died in 1810, so the chapel would have had to be built by someone else in the family. In the printed directories of the clergy of the dioceses of Tarnów, Kraków, and Przemyśl for 1824–1874, I did not find a priest named Podgórny.

One thing is certain. The chapel was built in the same year in which the children of house no. 5 began to have people from the manor in Dobra as godparents. In May 1826 my great-great-great-grandfather Jan Józef was held at the font by the representative of the lord of Dobra, and in 1831 his younger brother by Count Ludwik Małachowski himself, together with the countess. This is told in the story {{tytul:hrabia-przy-chrzcielnicy}}. A family with its own chapel was somebody in the area, and that is how the manor treated it.

A priest a hundred years later

There was a priest from the family after all, only a hundred years later. Andrzej Podgórni was born on December 19, 1892, at Jurków no. 108, to Antoni and Anna, née Chalcarz. In 1919 he joined the Dominicans and took the name Leon. He was ordained a priest in 1925. He survived the attack on the monastery in Podkamień in 1944 and died in Gidle in 1953. House no. 108 is the line of Melchior from house no. 5, so Father Leon was most likely a great-great-grandson of Jan and Katarzyna, just like my great-grandfather Józef. His life is described in the story {{tytul:ojciec-leon}}.

According to accounts passed down by local residents, about 50 meters from where the chapel stands there was once a tavern in which the outlaw chief Baczyński was captured in 1735. This is told in the story {{tytul:harnas}}.

How certain is this

  • CertainThe year the chapel was built, the bishops’ inscriptions of 1827 and 1848 on the slab of the portable altar, and the Podgórni families in houses no. 5 and 6. The entries were checked against the scans.
  • ProbableThat Father Leon Podgórni descended from Melchior of house no. 5.
  • PossibleThe tradition of a founder from the Podgórni family and of a son who became a priest, and that he was one of the sons of Jan and Katarzyna.

What we don’t know

  • Who built the chapel and which of the Podgórnis became a priest. The most could be learned from the records of the visitation of the parish of Dobra in July 1827, in the Diocesan Archive in Tarnów, and from the “Słownik biograficzny kapłanów diecezji tarnowskiej 1786–1985” (Biographical Dictionary of the Priests of the Diocese of Tarnów, 1786–1985).
  • Why relics of St. Valentine. A Walenty Podgórni (Walenty is the Polish form of Valentine) was born in house no. 6 in 1819, but that may be a coincidence.
