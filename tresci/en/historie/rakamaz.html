On June 23, 1853, in Rakamaz on the Tisza River, not far from Tokaj, Błażej Smoleń, a lodger from house no. 51 in Chyszówki, died. His wife, Marianna, née Janas, had stayed at home. The news reached the parish only on October 27, and that is when the priest added Błażej to the death register.

The priest placed in the volume an official extract from the death register of the parish of Rakamaz in Szabolcs County. In it the Hungarian parish priest called Błażej “peregrinus Slavus,” a wandering Slav from Dobra. He died of a fever, without the sacraments, and two days later he was buried there. According to the Hungarian extract he was 50, while the priest in Chyszówki gave his age as 74.

Rakamaz lies about 200 kilometers from Chyszówki as the crow flies, and farther over the mountains. What a lodger from the foot of Mogielica mountain was doing there, the registers do not say. A lodger had no land of his own and lived by working for others. Perhaps he went to Hungary to earn money, like many poor people from the mountains.

Others whom death found far from home

  • 1873 · ViennaJan Zawada of house no. 15 in Chyszówki, a 26-year-old uhlan, died of consumption in the military hospital in Vienna.
  • 1912 · PrussiaJan Kołodziej of house no. 106 in Jurków, a laborer, died in Prussia and was buried there. He was 30 and left a wife, Kunegunda. scan ↗
  • 1914 · on the Nida RiverJózef Smoleń from Chyszówki, a soldier of the 20th Infantry Regiment, was killed on November 27, 1914.
  • 1917 · RomaniaStanisław Tomera from Chyszówki was killed at the age of 19 near Capotesti. He was entered in the register only in 1925.
  • 1918 · Italian frontJózef Smoleń from Chyszówki (surname uncertainly read) was killed on July 4, 1918. The priest copied the German notice: “an der ital. Front den Heldentod fand,” died a hero’s death on the Italian front.

What we don’t know

  • Why Błażej went to Hungary, and whether he went alone.
  • How old he really was.
  • Whether the original record survives in the parish of Rakamaz.
